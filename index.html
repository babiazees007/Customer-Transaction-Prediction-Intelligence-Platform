Problem Statement & Banking Context

Why identifying future transactions drives high-impact business decisions.

The Business Challenge

In traditional banking operations, marketing and advisory outreach is reactive—offered only after a customer executes or fails to execute a transaction. By predicting whether a customer will conduct a transaction in advance, Santander can:

  • ✓ Pre-allocate Liquidity: Optimize reserve balances across branch and digital payment channels.
  • ✓ Personalized Credit Offers: Pre-approve credit lines right when transactional intent is peaking.
  • ✓ Proactive Retention: Flag non-transacting customers for targeted retention campaigns before churn occurs.

Task 3 Deliverable

Report on Challenges Faced & Engineering Solutions

Rigorous breakdown of real-world hurdles encountered and the technical solutions applied.

CHALLENGE 01

Severe Class Imbalance (8.95 : 1)

Only 10.05% of customers execute transactions. A dummy model predicting 0 gets 89.95% accuracy while failing 100% of transaction opportunities.

Technique Applied: Enforced ROC-AUC optimization, StratifiedKFold sampling, scale_pos_weight=2.0, and F1-score threshold tuning.
CHALLENGE 02

Anonymity & Semantic Blindness

All 200 features are named var_0 to var_199 without domain labels (like balance or income), preventing domain-specific ratio calculations.

Technique Applied: Leveraged statistical moments profiling (skewness, kurtosis) and non-parametric tree quantile splitting on numeric intervals.
CHALLENGE 03

Orthogonal & Uncorrelated Space

Pairwise correlation across all 19,900 feature pairs was virtually 0 ($r \approx 0.0018$). Standard polynomial interaction terms ($A \times B$) fail to provide lift.

Technique Applied: Exploited feature independence via Gaussian Naive Bayes (~0.889 AUC) and randomized feature fraction bagging in LightGBM.
CHALLENGE 04

Compute & Memory Footprint

200,000 rows × 202 columns equates to 40.4 million 64-bit floats (~308 MB), easily ballooning to 1.5+ GB during matrix manipulation and CV folds.

Technique Applied: Numeric downcasting to float32 reduced RAM by 49.5%, combined with LightGBM's 256-bin histogram algorithm.
